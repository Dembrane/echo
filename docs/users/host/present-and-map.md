# Present & Map for hosts

"Present" is where you prepare what the room sees and open the screen for it. "Map" is where
you explore the arguments people made. Both are marked Beta. Together they replace the old
library of views and aspects, which
[no longer exists](../../features/library-and-analysis.md).

| You want to… | Use |
|---|---|
| Show a room what people are saying while the session runs | "Present" |
| Have results ready at a set time | "Ready by", beside "Present" |
| Check or reword a result before anyone sees it | "Review results", on the "Present" page |
| Share results with people who have no login | "Public page", under "Share" |
| Explore every argument in the project | "Map" |

## Present to a room

1. Open your project and choose "Present" in the sidebar. The presentation is made the first
   time someone who can edit the project opens this page.
2. In the "Presentation editor", open "Outcomes" and choose what your audience can explore.
3. Press "Present". The room screen opens in a new tab and popcorn goes live. It stays live for
   8 hours unless you chose another time. A project that is already live or booked stays as it
   is.
4. Put that tab on the big screen and click through its opening pages. The last one has the
   button "Start popcorn →". It starts the slides after a countdown.

"Start popcorn →" and the waiting stage that follows are on the room screen. They are not steps
in the dashboard.

The outcomes you can show:

| Outcome | What the room sees |
|---|---|
| "Popcorn" | Short phrases from the conversations. Always on, so the room has something to read while the rest gets ready. |
| "Tensions" | Different perspectives and trade-offs: two sides of one question, and what there is to resolve. |
| "Map" | The argument map of the project. |
| "Stakeholders" | People, groups and what matters to them. |

popcorn is built for speed, not accuracy. A phrase in quotation marks is what someone said,
word for word. The others paraphrase.

## Go live, or book a start

While popcorn is live it reads the project's conversations every 2 minutes and works on the
ones that changed. The status line shows it, for example "Live until 17:00".

The arrow beside "Present" gives you more ways to present:

- "Stay live for": "1 hour", "8 hours" or "24 hours".
- "Ready by", while you are not live yet: give the time you want results by and press "Book".
  The first read starts 15 minutes before that time. A time that has already passed today
  means tomorrow. "Cancel" drops the booking.
- "Stop live", while you are live, ends the live reads.

When a booked read is in, everyone who can open the project gets an "Inbox" notification:
"Your results are ready to review".

> [!TIP]
> Plenary at 15:00? Book "Ready by" 15:00. The first read starts at 14:45, so you can look
> through the results before you put them on the screen.

Two more things happen on their own. Each time a conversation finishes, one read follows about
a minute later, live or not. And live ends by itself after three failed reads in a row.

## Review results before they show

Below the editor, "Review results" lists the findings in your presentation. For each one:

- "Edit" rewords it. You say what you changed ("A typo", "Clearer words, same meaning" or "The
  meaning") and why.
- "Hide from this presentation" keeps it off this presentation only. "Show again" brings it
  back.
- "Withdraw" takes it out of the analysis altogether, and off the map. It asks why, in one
  sentence. "Undo withdrawal" brings it back.
- "History / details" shows each change with who made it, when and why. "Use this wording"
  restores an earlier text.

The audience never sees the history, and never sees a finding you hid or withdrew.

When newer results exist, "Use latest results" brings them into the presentation. "Open in
Analysis" opens the [Analysis](../../features/analysis.md) page, which lists every finding of
the project with its evidence.

When do your changes reach the screen?

- Nobody is watching: "Changes show on the room screen as you make them."
- You are live, the public page is on, or the room screen is open: changes wait until you press
  "Show them". The "Audience preview" marks them "Not on the room screen yet".

The page notices an open room screen only when it was opened in the same browser. A screen on
another computer is not noticed. While you are live or the public page is on, changes always
wait. New results from a read reach an open screen without your help.

## The room screen

- It starts with the opening pages you switched on ("Introduction", "Data policy", "Take
  part"), then shows one tab per outcome.
- Before the first phrase it waits, with a line such as "waiting for the first conversation" or
  "reading the conversations…". A signed-in host who can edit the project also sees "Analyse
  now" there, which starts one read.
- It has controls for play and pause, previous and next slide, fullscreen, and a light or dark
  screen.
- It never shows transcripts.
- It needs a browser that is signed in to dembrane with access to the project. An account is
  signed in on [one browser at a time](./account-and-settings.md). For people without a login,
  use the public page.

You set the screen up in the tabs of the "Presentation editor":

| Tab | What you set |
|---|---|
| "Intro" and "Data policy" | "Presentation title", "Show introduction", "Show a disclosure", "Show a frame" and "Show what happens to the data" |
| "Host guide" | "Show the host guide": a screen with the code to take part and the steps from your host guide |
| "Outcomes" | Which outcomes the audience can explore |
| "Language" | "Follow project language", or your own "Screen language" and a translation of the results. "Popcorn also in" adds up to three more languages |
| "Appearance" | "Names on the legend", the "QR code" to add a voice, and the "made with dembrane" mark |

"Names on the legend" is off to start with and numbers the conversations. Switched on, it
shows the name typed on the phone, which can be a person's name. Taking the "made with
dembrane" mark off needs the Changemaker plan.

## The public page

Press "Share" and switch on "Public page": "Anyone with the link can watch. No login, and no
transcripts."

- You need the "Admin" role, and the workspace needs the Innovator plan or higher.
- With the switch on you get a QR code, "Copy link", "Open link", "Download QR code", "Copy
  embed code" and "Download event printouts".
- The link works only while the switch is on. Switching off and on again gives a new link, and
  the old one stays closed. A closed link shows "This presentation is not available."

## Read the Map

The map reads the project's transcripts, finds the arguments people make and places related
arguments close together.

1. Choose "Map" in the sidebar and press "Generate map". There is nothing to fill in. It reads
   every conversation that has a transcript.
2. Wait while it reads. You can leave the page. The sidebar entry shows "Ready" when the map is
   done.
3. Click a point. "Spotlight" shows the argument with its "Quotes" and "Connections". Each
   quote links to the conversation it came from.

How to read it:

- Each point is one argument: a short statement with one to five word-for-word quotes. The
  header counts them, for example "42 arguments from 12 conversations".
- There are two views of the same arguments, "Cluster map" and "Argument tree". Show them "One
  at a time" or "Side by side".
- "Color nodes by" colours the points by conversation (the default), tag, valence or factual
  status. Valence is "Positive", "Negative", "Neutral" or "Not assessed".
- "Filter" narrows the map by tag or by conversation.
- Rest the cursor on a group of three or more points until the circle closes. The cluster is
  saved and a language model gives it a title. Saved clusters also show on the room screen and
  the public page.

Claims and opinions:

- A claim is a statement about the world that evidence could back up or contradict. Anything
  else (a stance, a preference, a personal story) is labelled "Opinion".
- "Fact-check this claim" in "Spotlight" has a language model search the web. The result is
  "Likely true", "Likely false", "Contested" or "Inconclusive", with a written justification
  and links to its sources. Read the sources before you repeat a result.
- Opinions are not fact-checked.

Good to know:

- There is one map per project. "Regenerate" makes it again. If that fails, you keep the
  previous map.
- A map is also made on its own when a presentation includes the "Map" outcome and the project
  has none yet.
- The "Map" page has no edit controls. Reword or withdraw a finding under "Review results" on
  "Present", and the map follows.
- Tensions are not drawn on the "Map" page. They are the "Tensions" outcome of a presentation.

## Who can do what

| Action | Role |
|---|---|
| Open "Present", the room screen and "Map" | Everyone on the workspace except "Billing" |
| Edit a presentation, go live, review results, generate a map, fact-check | "Admin", "Member", "External" |
| Switch on "Public page" for a presentation | "Admin", on the Innovator plan or higher |

Apart from the public page and the "made with dembrane" mark, none of this needs a paid plan.
