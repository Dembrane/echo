# Your report

After the conversations, your host can write a report with dembrane and publish it. It is one
report about the whole project, the same for everyone who opens it. It is not a summary of your
own conversation, and it contains no transcripts.

Not every host publishes one.

## How you get it

- By email. If the thank-you page asks "Do you want to stay in the loop?", type your email
  address, press "Add", then "Submit". When your host publishes a report, you get an email titled
  "A report featuring your input is ready", with a "View report" button. A group can add several
  addresses.
- By link or QR code, if your host shares one.

You do not need an account to read it.

## What you see

An article in questions and answers, with an introduction and a conclusion. A language model
writes it from the project's conversations, and your host can edit the text.

If nothing is published yet, the page says: "This report is not yet available." Check back
later, or ask your host.

The link shows the project's latest published report, so it can change when your host publishes
a new one.

## Stop the emails

Every report email has an unsubscribe link. It opens "Unsubscribe from notifications". Press
"Unsubscribe", and you see "You have successfully unsubscribed."
