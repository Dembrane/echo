## What the words mean

The file defines three words. Read them before you decide which side of the line you are on.

- "Total Finances" means "the largest of your aggregate gross revenues, entire budget, and/or funding (no matter the source)".
- "you" and "your" include "any individual or entity agreeing to these terms and any affiliates of such individual or entity". The finances of affiliates count.
- "production" means "any use other than (i) development of (including evaluation of the Licensed Work), debugging, or testing your offerings, or (ii) making the Licensed Work available standalone in unmodified object code form".

## What you may do

The licence grants "the right to copy, modify, create derivative works, redistribute, and make non-production use of the Licensed Work".

Production use is allowed inside the Additional Use Grant above. If your use does not comply, the file says "you must purchase a commercial license from the Licensor, its affiliated entities, or authorized resellers, or you must refrain from using the Licensed Work".

## When a version becomes GPL v3

The change happens "on the Change Date, or the fourth anniversary of the first publicly available distribution of a specific version of the Licensed Work under this License, whichever comes first". From then on that version is available under GPL v3, and the rights granted under the Business Source License end.

The licence applies separately to each version, and the Change Date can differ per version.
