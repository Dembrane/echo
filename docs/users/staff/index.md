# For staff

This guide is for people who work at dembrane. It covers the staff console: the part of the
dashboard where you look after customers' accounts, plans and billing. Customers do not see it.

New to the console? Start with [the staff console](./admin-panel-overview.md).

## Find your job

| You want to | Read |

| Find your way around the console | [The staff console](./admin-panel-overview.md) |
| See what each account uses and pays, or follow a payment | [Usage, billing and payments](./usage-and-billing-rollup.md) |
| Change a tier, set a discount or mark a partner | [Tiers and discounts](./discounts-trials-and-tiers.md) |
| Bill a customer by invoice | [Managed billing](./managed-and-offline-billing.md) |
| Build a demo, send an offer, follow signing, tasks and questions | [Customer accounts](./customer-accounts.md) |
