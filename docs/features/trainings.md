# Trainings

A training is a session that dembrane gives to people who use dembrane in high-risk
settings. Everyone who completes one holds a licence for one year.

The dashboard has no training screen. dembrane sets a training up for your organisation,
schedules it and records who attended. To arrange one, ask dembrane. There are two
formats: online (foundational, two hours) and in person (advanced, four hours, on site).

What you will notice:

- When dembrane marks your training complete, you get the notice "Your training is
  complete": "You now hold a one-year license to use dembrane in high-risk settings."
- The licence ends 365 days after the day you completed the training.
- A licence belongs to a person, not to a workspace. It is separate from your
  [plan](./tiers-and-billing.md), and it does not unlock or block any feature.
- When you first sign in, dembrane asks: "Do you plan to use dembrane in health, education,
  recruitment, critical infrastructure management, law enforcement or justice contexts?" If
  you answer yes, it asks: "Have you completed a training?"
