# Feature catalogue

This is the reference for what dembrane does. Each page covers one capability: what it is,
when you would reach for it, which role and plan it needs, and how it works. If you would
rather read from your own point of view, start with *[who you are](../users/index.md)*. Those
guides link back here.

New to dembrane? *[5 ways to use dembrane](./five-ways-to-use-dembrane.md)* shows where it
fits in a dialogue or deliberative process.

## Foundations

How dembrane is organised, who can do what, and what each plan includes.

- *[Organisations & workspaces](./organisations-and-workspaces.md)*: where your people and
  projects live.
- *[Roles & permissions](./roles-and-permissions.md)*: owner, admin, member, billing,
  external and observer, and what each can do.
- *[Tiers & billing](./tiers-and-billing.md)*: the plans, the free plan's limits, seats, and
  what needs a paid plan.
- *[Offers & invoices](./offers-and-invoices.md)*: how your organisation gets an offer,
  signs it and receives its invoice.
- *[Invites & access](./invites-and-access.md)*: how people join an organisation or a
  workspace.
- *[Visibility & discovery](./visibility-and-discovery.md)*: who can find and join a
  workspace.
- *[Account & security](./account-and-security.md)*: signing in, two-factor authentication
  and your own settings.
- *[Staff support access](./staff-support-access.md)*: how dembrane staff reach a workspace,
  and only when you allow it.

## Collecting conversations

Getting what people say into dembrane.

- *[Projects](./projects.md)*: a set of conversations and everything made from them.
- *[Recording](./recording.md)*: recording in the portal and uploading audio.
- *[Transcription](./transcription.md)*: how audio becomes text.
- *[The live monitor](./live-monitor.md)* (Beta): watch a session while it runs.
- *[The participant portal](./portal-and-participant-experience.md)*: the page people
  record on, without an account.
- *[The portal editor](./portal-editor.md)*: every setting a host has for that page.
- *[dembrane Go](./mobile-app-dembrane-go.md)*: the iOS app, which cannot sign in to the
  current version of dembrane.

## Making sense of it

Turning hours of dialogue into something a group can act on.

- *[Conversations & transcripts](./conversations-and-transcripts.md)*: find, read and tidy
  what you collected.
- *[Chat & Ask](./chat-and-ask.md)*: ask questions about the conversations in a project.
- *[Present](./present.md)* (Beta): prepare what a room sees, with popcorn, Tensions, Map
  and Stakeholders.
- *[Map](./argument-map.md)* (Beta): the arguments people make, with related arguments
  close together.
- *[Analysis](./analysis.md)*: every finding with its evidence and the history of its edits.
- *[Canvases](./canvas.md)* (Beta): a live page built from recent conversations.
- *[Reports](./reports.md)*: one written article made from a project's conversations.
- *[Library & analysis](./library-and-analysis.md)*: removed in dembrane v3. This page says
  what does that job now.

## Working with others

- *[Trainings](./trainings.md)*: what a dembrane training means for you today.
