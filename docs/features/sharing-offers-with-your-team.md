# Sharing an offer with your team

A decision about dembrane is rarely one person's. On your organisation's "Overview",
"Who is involved" lists the colleagues who read the offer and the DPA with you, and what
each of them does in the decision. Add someone with "Add someone", the "+" next to the
pictures at the top of the page, or "Share with a colleague" on any document.

## Who can add whom

| You are | You can add |
|---|---|
| Owner or admin | Anyone: a member of your organisation, or someone new by email |
| Billing | Members of your organisation |
| A colleague who was added | Members of your organisation |

Someone new gets an email invitation. They confirm their name, get a code by email (no
password), and land on the organisation's "Overview". If you may not invite someone new,
the dialog says so: "Only an owner or admin can invite someone who is not a member yet."

When the address is not on your own email domain, the dialog says so under the field and
again before you send. It is a reminder to check the address, nothing more.

## What someone does

When you add someone, you can say what they do. Choose any number, or none:

- "Gives the go-ahead"
- "Will run the rollout"
- "Checks IT and security"
- "Checks privacy and AI rules"
- "Handles contract and purchasing"
- "Represents staff or stakeholders"
- "Other", in your own words

With nothing chosen, they are kept informed. The people who sign or say what they do lead
the list; everyone else is under "Other members". Owners and admins also see the members
who are "not added yet" there, with one link to add them.

When nobody covers something that often holds a decision up, the list says so, one line
at a time, for example "Nobody checks privacy and AI rules yet." Choose "Add someone" to
fill it, or "Not needed" (owners, admins and Billing).

A colleague you add is asked once, on their first visit, whether what you chose is right.
They can say yes or change it.

## Who signs

Who signs the offer and who signs the DPA is set in the same dialog, under "Signing", and
in the task "Tell us who signs". A blue "Signs the offer" or "Signs the DPA" marker shows
who it is. Only owners and admins choose or change who signs; Billing signs themselves or
reminds the person already named. One person signs each document, so choosing someone else
asks first: "Change who signs the offer?" The person who had it no longer can sign.

## What a colleague sees

A colleague you add reads the offer and the DPA, and the tasks about them, with the
answers and our notes. They do not see invoices, billing details or questions to us, and
they cannot sign, answer tasks or download a document until it is signed. They can add
other members of the organisation.

## Taking someone out

Open the "..." menu next to their name and choose "Remove" with their name. If they had
not joined yet, their invitation is withdrawn and the link we sent stops working. If they
had joined,
they stay a member of your organisation, but can no longer read the offer and the DPA.
Owners, admins and Billing are in by their role; change their role on the "Members" page.
