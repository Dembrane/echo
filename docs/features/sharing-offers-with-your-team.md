# Sharing an offer with your team

A decision about dembrane is rarely one person's. On your organisation's "Overview",
"Involved in procurement" lists the colleagues who read the offer and the DPA with you,
and what each of them does in the decision. Add someone with "Add someone", the "+" next
to the pictures at the top of the page, or "Share with a colleague" on the offer or the
DPA under "Documents". The "DPA" label shows its full name when you hover or tap it: "DPA:
data processing agreement".

## Who can add whom

| You are | You can add |
|---|---|
| Owner or admin | Anyone: a member of your organisation, or someone new by email |
| Billing | Members of your organisation |
| A colleague who was added | Members of your organisation |

Someone new gets an email invitation. They confirm their name, get a code by email (no
password), and land on the organisation's "Overview". If the code does not come, the
sign-in step says what to do: "No code after a minute? Check your spam folder, or ask the
person who shared this with you to add you." If you may not invite someone new, the
dialog says so: "Only an owner or admin can invite someone who is not a member yet. You
can add a colleague who is already a member."

An owner or admin can also invite them from the "Members" page, in the role they choose.
That invitation is theirs: it stays when you take the person out of the room or choose
someone else to sign.

When the address is not on your own email domain, the dialog says so under the field and
again before you send. It is a reminder to check the address, nothing more.

## What someone does

When you add someone, you can say what they do. Choose any number, or none:

- "Gives the go-ahead"
- "Will run the rollout"
- "Checks IT and security"
- "Checks privacy and AI rules"
- "Handles contract"
- "Represents staff or stakeholders"
- "Other", in your own words

With nothing chosen, they are kept informed. The people who sign or say what they do lead
the list; everyone else is under "Other members". Owners, admins and Billing also see the
members who are "not added yet" there, each with one link: "Involve {first} in
procurement".

When nobody covers something that often holds a decision up, the list says so, one line
at a time, for example "Nobody checks privacy and AI rules yet." Choose "Add someone" to
fill it, or "Not needed" (owners, admins and Billing).

A colleague you add is asked once, on their first visit, whether what you chose is right.
They can say "Yes, that is right" or "Change" it.

Anyone in the list can change what they do themselves, at any time: open the "..." menu
on your own row and choose "Change what I do". The dialog asks "What do you do?". To change
what someone else does, choose "Change what {first} does" in their menu (whoever runs the
account, or the person who added them).

## Who signs

Who signs the offer and who signs the DPA is set in the same dialog, under "Signing", and
in the task "Tell us who signs the offer". A blue "Signs the offer" or "Signs the DPA"
marker shows who it is. Only owners and admins choose or change who signs; Billing signs
themselves or reminds the person already named. One person signs each document, so
choosing someone else asks first: "Change who signs the offer?" The person who had it can
no longer sign: the link we sent them stops working, and the invitation to your
organisation that came with it is withdrawn.

dembrane can name who signs when it sends the offer. The task "Tell us who signs the
offer" then starts from that person.

## What a colleague sees

A colleague you add reads the offer and the DPA. Under "Documents" each document shows its
tools as icons, each named when you hover it: "Open to sign", "Read", "Download PDF", and
"Share with a colleague" on the offer and the DPA. A colleague opens a document with
"Read"; "Download PDF" appears once it is signed. Under "Tasks", "On the offer and the DPA"
lists the tasks about them, read only, with the answers and our notes. Each says what it
is about, such as "Who signs the offer and the DPA", not what to do.

They do not see invoices, billing details or questions to us, and they cannot answer
tasks or sign, unless they are the person named to sign. They can add other members of
the organisation.

## Taking someone out

Open the "..." menu next to their name and choose "Remove {first}". If they had not
joined yet, their invitation is withdrawn and the link we sent stops working. If they had
joined, they stay a member of your organisation, but can no longer read the offer and the
DPA. Owners, admins and Billing are in by their role; change their role on the "Members"
page. You cannot take yourself out from your own row.
