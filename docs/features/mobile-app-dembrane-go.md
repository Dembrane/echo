# dembrane Go (the mobile app)

dembrane Go is dembrane's app for iPhone. It cannot sign in to the current version of
dembrane, so you cannot use it with your account.

## What to do instead

Record through the portal in your phone's browser. Nothing needs to be installed.

1. Open the project's "Overview" on a computer and scan the QR code in the "Take part" card
   with your phone. Or use "Copy link" and send the link to yourself.
2. Open the link in your phone's own browser, such as Safari or Chrome, and not inside
   another app.
3. Press "Record". Keep the screen on and the tab open until you press "Finish".

Reading transcripts, asking questions and writing reports all happen in the dashboard, in a
browser.
