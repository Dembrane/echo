# Reporting Security Issues

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, we encourage you to let us know right away.

We will investigate all legitimate reports and do our best to quickly fix the problem.

Email security@dembrane.com or use the [report form](https://dembrane.notion.site/cc3d257b14ea462ca8272c6837278eb0) to disclose any security vulnerabilities. Our disclosure policy is on the [trust page](https://trust.dembrane.com).
