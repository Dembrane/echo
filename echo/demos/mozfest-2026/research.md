# MozFest 2026: public context for a synthetic Popcorn demo

Research checked on 22 September 2026. This report is context, not evidence of anyone's views. No email or private material was used.

## Public facts

- MozFest 2026 runs from Wednesday 28 to Friday 30 October 2026 at Recinte Fabra i Coats in Barcelona, "a former industrial complex turned cultural powerhouse", in partnership with the City of Barcelona and as part of Open Tech Week. Source: [Mozilla Festival](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/festival/).
- The 2026 theme is Wilding: loosening rigid systems so communities and technologies can evolve collectively; community-owned mesh networks, federated platforms, open protocols, community archives, and tools built on transparency, trust and mutual flourishing. Source: [Mozilla Festival](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/festival/).
- The festival runs across eight community co-curated tracks: Wilding Advocacy, Wilding Knowledge, Wilding Systems, Wilding With Nature, Youth Wilding (ages 13 to 17), Wilding Creativity, Founders Wilding and Developers Wilding. Source: [Tracks](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/festival/tracks/).
- Formats include debates, labs and workshops, forums, showcases, talks and installations; the programme is co-created: people propose the sessions and host the conversations. Source: [Mozilla Festival](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/festival/).
- In 2025 more than 2,000 people came to Barcelona for the festival. Source: [Mozilla Festival](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/festival/).
- The venue is at Carrer de Sant Adrià 20, in Sant Andreu. Source: [Xarxa Punt TIC](https://punttic.gencat.cat/en/esdeveniment/mozilla-fest-2026). Fabra i Coats was a thread factory; the transcripts keep its history general and assert no dates.

## Scenario and uncertainty

The scenario is dembrane's, not MozFest's: listening infrastructure across the festival, a QR code in every track room, small harvest conversations recorded after sessions, and popcorn on the hallway screens. Nothing here says MozFest plans, endorses or has seen this. The festival has not happened yet; the demo imagines it.

## Fictional design choices

Eight invented conversations: one harvest per track, except Youth Wilding, plus an open recording booth by the café. Youth Wilding is left out deliberately. Inventing teenagers' voices for a sales demo would read badly, and a real deployment with minors needs guardian consent and its own flow. The booth has an adult youth-track facilitator reporting what the teenagers asked, and no minor speaks.

Languages: five rooms in English, Wilding Knowledge in Spanish, Wilding With Nature in Catalan. The deck is translated into English and Spanish.

Each room has its own character (heated, quiet with a gem, practical, constructive, playful drift, candid, technical, mosaic) and positions that collide across rooms: engaging platforms versus building alternatives; open by default versus knowledge that must stay closed; community data for models versus collective consent; efficiency versus sufficiency; growth versus steward ownership; openness versus safety; being heard versus being mined. The booth holds the sceptic's question, "why is an AI listening at a digital rights festival?", on purpose: a MozFest audience will ask it first, and the demo should show that popcorn surfaces that critique instead of hiding it.

Every person, project, collective, company, product and place-name initiative is invented. No real person is quoted or paraphrased, no real organisation is named, and no talk from the programme is referenced. Real places appear only as setting. The phrases, tensions and stakeholders come from dembrane's real popcorn pipeline reading these invented transcripts; they are not findings about anyone.
