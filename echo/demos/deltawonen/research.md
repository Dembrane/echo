# deltaWonen: public context for a fictional Popcorn demo

Research checked on 16 September 2026. This report is context, not evidence of stakeholder views.

## Public facts

- deltaWonen is a housing corporation active in Zwolle, Kampen and Oldebroek. Its 2024 annual report describes the course “Ruimte voor Geluk” in terms of woongeluk, leefgeluk and werkgeluk. Source: [Woongeluk, annual report 2024](https://publicaties.deltawonen.nl/jaarverslag-2024/woongeluk).
- The updated course was adopted in early 2024. Stakeholders are involved in developing and implementing it. Source: [Governance, annual report 2024](https://publicaties.deltawonen.nl/jaarverslag-2024/over-besturen-en-toezichthouden).
- Public reporting connects neighbourhood work and regional partnerships with housing and liveability. Source: [Thuis in de buurt, annual report 2024](https://publicaties.deltawonen.nl/jaarverslag-2024/thuis-in-de-buurt).
- The public visitation reflection discusses involving partners early enough. This is an organisational reflection, not permission to attribute invented opinions to those partners. Source: [Visitatie](https://deltawonen.nl/over-ons/reglementen-procedures/visitatie/).

## Sales brief and uncertainty

The host supplied the scenario: a Bondgenotendag exploring what geluk means and which organisational success pillars might express it. This is scenario metadata, not public evidence that this event occurred. Its date, attendees, precise agenda and outcomes have not been verified. Do not display an invented event date. The suggestion that the overall course runs until 2030 is unverified; 2030 appears in specific public policy targets and must not be treated as the end date of the whole course.

No email was accessed or incorporated. Private correspondence, if later connected, may inform a private sales brief but must not feed the public example while it claims public sources only.

## Fictional design choices

Five imaginary tables explore: a secure home, belonging in a neighbourhood, agency and trust, working together, and recognising progress. These are candidate lenses chosen for demonstration, not deltaWonen's official pillars and not findings about real people. Each conversation, phrase, tension and stakeholder relationship is wholly invented. There are no named real participants, quotations from real participants, measured response rates or invented commitments.

The purpose is to illustrate the listening experience. The real Bondgenotendag must author its own stories, meanings and decisions. The public sources ground organisational context only; they do not validate the synthetic perspectives.
