# Example Homes: context for a fictional Popcorn demo

Example Homes is an invented housing association, the English twin of Voorbeeld Wonen in `example/`. It stands in for a real organisation so the repository can carry a complete, reviewed example without naming anyone dembrane talks to.

## Scenario

A Partner Day where tenants, neighbourhood partners and staff explore what happiness means to them and how an organisation could recognise progress towards it. The event, its date and its attendees are imaginary.

## Fictional design choices

Five imaginary tables explore: a secure home, belonging in a neighbourhood, agency and trust, working together, and recognising progress. Each conversation, phrase, tension and stakeholder relationship is wholly invented. There are no named real participants, quotations from real people or claims about a real organisation.

The purpose is to illustrate the listening experience. A real event authors its own stories, meanings and decisions.
